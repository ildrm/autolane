# Creative direction concepts

**ID:** 06/01_CREATIVE_DIRECTION · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED; no trademark, asset or stakeholder approval · **Owner:** Design systems lead · **Reviewers:** Brand, accessibility, UX, frontend · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Evaluation criteria
The experience must convey trust and precision through original assets, with legible data and accessible interaction across consumer and staff workflows. The following are design proposals, not market-tested brands. Do not copy reference-company marks, layouts, photography or illustrations. Brand naming and trademark clearance remain decisions.

| Attribute | A — Quiet Precision (recommended) | B — Electric Circuit | C — Atelier Heritage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Artistic philosophy | Architectural composition, restrained contrast, confident details. | Energetic motion and high-contrast digital surfaces. | Editorial craft and understated material warmth. |
| Personality and palette | Reliable, modern; deep ink, ivory, teal accent. | Fast, adventurous; graphite, electric cyan, vivid orange. | Considered, familiar; charcoal, warm paper, copper. |
| Type and imagery | Geometric-humanist sans with generous numbers; natural light, three-quarter vehicle shots. | Technical grotesk and large numerals; night studio with bright edges. | Refined sans and editorial display serif; natural surroundings and texture. |
| Motif and motion | Fine alignment lines, cropped details; purposeful short state transitions. | Circuit traces and glows; energetic transitions. | Subtle framing and tactile surfaces; slow editorial reveals. |
| Density and access | Spacious public site; compact task-first operations; strong contrast and reduced-motion alternative. | Medium density; glow and saturated colors need careful contrast and epilepsy review. | Generous; serif and texture need small-text and low-vision tests. |
| Complexity/performance | Medium; photography + CSS, optional 3D only when justified. | High due to effects and animation; highest GPU and battery impact. | Medium; imagery and font pairing require asset discipline. |
| Strength/weakness | Broad trust and usability; risks looking restrained without strong art direction. | Distinctive in campaigns; risks distraction in a live auction. | Premium editorial character; risks appearing less digital in a busy dashboard. |

**Recommendation:** Quiet Precision. Specify the same trust cues across surfaces while allowing staff density and restrained motion. Use accent sparingly for primary actions and focus, never as the sole status signal. Production approval requires naming search, original photography rights, accessibility contrast tests and a comparative prototype study. The recommended visual direction is independent of any transaction, warranty or legal commitment.

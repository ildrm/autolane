# Brand visual language

**ID:** 06/02_BRAND_VISUAL_LANGUAGE · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED; no trademark, asset or stakeholder approval · **Owner:** Design systems lead · **Reviewers:** Brand, accessibility, UX, frontend · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Identity and intent
**PROPOSED working identity:** “Northline” is a temporary project name for design exploration, not a cleared trademark or approved public name. Verify availability, linguistic suitability and domain ownership before use. Use a distinctive typographic wordmark with a directional line motif; do not create or imply an official logo from this specification. Northline signifies clarity and confident movement, not vehicle ownership or payment assurance.

## Art direction
Photography is original or appropriately licensed: wide, natural light, neutral background, camera at vehicle beltline with three-quarter exterior, then consistent front/rear, cabin, wheels and defect details. Capture an honest condition record; no retouching that removes defects or alters color. Product imagery should be contextual but not interfere with a readable headline. Obtain rights, model/property releases, metadata provenance and expiration before publication. Default ratio 16:9 hero, 4:3 card, 1:1 detail; use responsive crops and an accurate neutral placeholder.

Graphic motifs are fine directional rules, frame corners and an abstract path; no speed lines behind live price data. Icons use simple 24-unit line geometry, 2-unit stroke, rounded joins, consistent optical weight and an accompanying text label when meaning is not universally clear. Diagrams use a neutral vehicle outline with numbered finding markers, color plus text severity and a matching accessible list. Illustration is secondary to real condition evidence; explicitly label representative illustrations.

## Writing and voice
Use plain, measured, direct English. Example: “Arrange an inspection” rather than “Unlock a seamless journey.” Show assumptions and next steps, do not assert sale completion after an accepted bid. Public tone is welcoming; operations tone is concise and factual. Layout and language follow LTR; locale formatting, currency and timezone remain configurable. Review all claims with legal and operations.

## Acceptance
Compare logo, motifs, layouts and imagery against source reference assets for original expression; record image licensing; check contrast and alt text on every crop; validate brand concept with sellers and dealers before approval.

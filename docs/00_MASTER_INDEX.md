**ID:** DOC-00 · **Package revision:** 0.3 · **Status:** proposed documentation, not approved for live operation · **Owner:** Documentation architect · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Start here

This repository specifies an original English-first, LTR managed automotive marketplace. The initial loop is seller intake → booking → inspection → valuation → approved-dealer auction → explicit seller decision → contract coordination → external settlement verification → title/custody evidence → closure. Consumer inventory, sourcing, financing, logistics, claims, CMS, growth and intelligence have separate domain boundaries and staged activation. No application or deployment has been implemented.

Evidence classifications remain VERIFIED (the source says it, not independent audit), INFERRED, PROPOSED, UNVERIFIED and DECISION REQUIRED. A virtual role does not imply a human signature. Documentation validation, software verification and commercial/legal authorization are independent gates. All prior discovery batch counts are historical; the manifest records current artifact paths and hashes.

## Governance and decision register

All statuses are **PROPOSED** or **DECISION REQUIRED**, not approvals. A decision owner is the role accountable for approving it, not evidence that a person has signed off.

| ID | Priority | Decision / recommended provisional posture | Owner | Required to unblock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-01 | P0 | Launch jurisdiction, regulator applicability and contracting entities: choose before final legal, identity, tax and transfer requirements. | Executive sponsor + legal | Legal scope and policy baseline |
| DEC-02 | P0 | Commercial model and vehicle title: recommend explore intermediary coordination first, without presuming inventory ownership; select liability, contracting parties and risk allocation. | Product + legal | Contracts, consumer protection, accounting |
| DEC-03 | P0 | Money flow and custody: recommend external regulated provider or participant-to-participant settlement until assessed; decide who holds, releases, refunds and reconciles funds. | Finance + legal | Payment integration, ledger and launch |
| DEC-04 | P0 | Binding auction rules: eligibility, reserve disclosure, bid increments, timestamp authority, tie handling, closing/extension, seller response period, cancellation and default remedies. | Commercial operations + legal | Auction contracts, state machines and UAT |
| DEC-05 | P0 | Contract and ownership-transfer requirements including identity and signature evidence; depend on jurisdiction and transaction model. | Legal + operations | Contract templates and transaction release |
| DEC-06 | P0 | Inspection responsibility, publication/correction, warranty scope and dispute remedies; no warranty is assumed. | Inspection lead + legal | Inspection rules and customer terms |
| DEC-07 | P0 | Privacy, consent, retention, access and applicable breach obligations for jurisdiction and data categories. | Privacy + security | Data lifecycle and launch approval |
| DEC-08 | P1 | Launch region, branch capacity, baseline volume and staffing; current scale and estimates are not known. | Operations + product | Capacity model and delivery estimate |
| DEC-09 | P1 | Technology choices and service boundaries; use the consistency and operational risks above as evaluation criteria rather than importing the reference stack. | Architecture | ADRs and technical plans |
| DEC-10 | P1 | Optional proxy bidding, anti-sniping, deposits, dealer credit and automated valuation; keep disabled until each rule and risk is approved. | Product + commercial operations | Scoped future requirements |

# Wireframe and screen behavior specifications

**ID:** 05/05_WIREFRAME_SPECIFICATIONS · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED, subject to research and stakeholder review · **Owner:** UX lead · **Reviewers:** Product, accessibility, operations, engineering, legal · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Shared screen-state contract
The [screen inventory](04_SCREEN_INVENTORY.md) is the authoritative route/ID list. On desktop place a single H1, brief purpose, status/primary action, main work area and contextual support in that order. Tablet stacks secondary rail under main content; mobile renders one column with a non-obscuring primary action. Loading retains a labeled skeleton or “Loading…” and page structure; empty states explain the next permitted action; failures preserve input, display what failed and Retry; 403 uses generic access copy and a support route; success appears only after server confirmation. Focus moves to the error summary or success heading, and interactive controls expose programmatic labels. Standard text must wrap at 320px and 200% zoom. Do not use decorative motion to convey an outcome.

## Remaining screen families
Authentication uses labeled identifier, verification, recovery and support; avoid account enumeration. Public informational, branch and editorial pages use accessible heading hierarchy, canonical policy and approved content. Dealer application and status distinguish submitted, pending, approved and rejected without promising approval. Staff admin, support, content and reporting use scoped tables and detail panes, server pagination, accessible filters, immutable action history and permission-appropriate controls. Every screen in SCR-001–048 inherits this contract; the screen/API binding matrix provides its exact fields and supporting operation IDs, with domain-specific panel states and schema constraints.

## Quality and instrumentation
PROPOSED public field targets: LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1 at p75, segmented mobile/desktop, based on [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals); these are targets, not measurements. Analytics events are proposed names and must be registered by the analytics owner, consent-aware and devoid of phone, identity, VIN or bid amounts unless expressly authorized. Record form start/complete, booking confirmed, bid result category and offer decision; no impression is a confirmed business event. Verify keyboard, reduced motion, 320px, slow network, revoked role and all error states for each screen family.

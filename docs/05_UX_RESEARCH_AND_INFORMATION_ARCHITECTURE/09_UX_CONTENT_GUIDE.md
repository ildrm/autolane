# UX content guide

**ID:** 05/09_UX_CONTENT_GUIDE · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED, subject to research and stakeholder review · **Owner:** UX lead · **Reviewers:** Product, accessibility, operations, engineering, legal · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Voice and writing rules
Write in natural English, active voice and sentence case. Be precise about uncertainty, next actions, amounts, timezone and ownership. Do not promise a sale, financing, cleared payment, transfer or warranty unless established by the relevant policy and verified state. Use “seller,” “dealer,” “inspection,” “offer,” “bid” and “transaction” consistently. Avoid internal IDs, legal jargon and blame. Use exact ISO currency and local date/time with timezone where relevant; prices must distinguish estimates, bids, fees and final obligations.

## Reusable copy
| Situation | Heading | Body/action |
|---|---|---|
| Empty seller cases | No vehicles yet | Start a selling request to arrange an inspection. **Start selling** |
| Appointment held | Appointment confirmed | Your inspection is scheduled for [date, time, timezone] at [branch]. **View appointment** |
| Slot conflict | This time is no longer available | Your details are saved. Choose another available time. **Choose a time** (say saved only when confirmed) |
| Pending offer | No offer to review yet | We will notify you when an offer is ready. **View case** |
| Decision | Review this offer | [Amount and currency]. Your decision does not by itself complete a sale. **Accept offer** / **Reject offer** |
| Bid uncertain | Checking your bid | We have not confirmed the result. Check your bid history before trying again. **View bid history** |
| Outbid | Your bid is no longer highest | Review the current amount and the auction rules before bidding again. **Review auction** |
| No access | You do not have access to this page | If you think this is a mistake, contact support. **Contact support** |
| Settlement pending | Payment is being verified | Your transaction remains open until the required checks are complete. **View details** |
| Provider outage | Financing status unavailable | We could not get an update from the provider. Try again later or contact support. **Try again** |

## Errors, notifications and review
Say what happened and what the user can do. Focus errors on correction, e.g., “Enter a valid mileage” rather than “Invalid input.” Notifications must identify the vehicle by a privacy-safe reference, link to a signed-in destination, state date/time and avoid VIN, identity documents, sensitive financial amounts or full contact details in untrusted channels. Legal approves terms and substantive financial copy; UX reviews reading level, accessibility and consistency; locale and jurisdiction review date, tax and monetary formats. Acceptance: each status has a plain-language next step and owner; test comprehension of seller acceptance versus completed sale with participants.

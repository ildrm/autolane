## Market selection precedes legal design
**DECISION REQUIRED:** Launch jurisdiction and contracting entities. No local legal conclusion can be made from the choice of English UI. The table is an issue-spotting inventory, not legal advice or a compliance determination.

| Topic | Questions for qualified local counsel and finance | Work blocked until resolved |
|---|---|---|
| Marketplace/intermediary | Is licensing needed, who is principal, what fiduciary/consumer duties and disclosures apply? | Contract, fee, claims and market launch. |
| Auctions | Are bids binding; how are reserves, cancellation, ties, extensions and default governed? | Auction terms and production bidding. |
| Payments and custody | May the platform receive or transmit funds; are segregated accounts, regulated partners or escrow required? | Payment integrations, settlement and refunds. |
| Vehicle title | What documents, authorization, liens, transfer, tax and handover sequence are required? | Ownership states and closure criteria. |
| Privacy and identity | Lawful basis, identity requirements, sensitive data, international transfers, retention and breach reporting? | Data collection, sharing and deletion policies. |
| Consumer rights and warranties | Mandatory remedies, inspection statements, unfair terms, complaint channels and cooling-off rights? | Warranty copy, contract and dispute SLA. |
| Finance and referrals | Lending/broker licenses, eligibility, disclosures, consent and commissions? | Credit and lender integrations. |
| Signatures and records | Permitted e-signature, proof of consent, audit retention, legal holds and admissibility? | Electronic contracting and evidence lifecycle. |
| Tax and invoicing | Taxable services, payer, invoice and withholding, currency/rounding and timing? | Fee quote and accounting policies. |
| Advertising and accessibility | Accuracy of price/availability, marketing permissions and applicable accessibility obligations? | Public claims and launch gate. |

## Approval evidence
For each jurisdiction keep jurisdiction, question, cited law/guidance, counsel name, advice date, affected feature, decision, control, owner and next review date. A permission or provider contract must be confirmed before activities begin. Escalate conflicts between laws and model to sponsor; do not assume a configurable field alone creates compliance.

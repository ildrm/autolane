## Research protocol and open issues
Validate geography, eligibility, complete terms, inventory channels, fee payer, guarantees and dates before claiming direct comparability. Recruit sellers, dealers and branch staff in the chosen market; test trust and willingness to pay using neutral concept prototypes. No market share, relative conversion, competitor price advantage or ranking has been verified. Acceptance: every competitor assertion has a current source, dates and feature-specific caveats, and any conclusion is identified as an inference or proposed hypothesis.

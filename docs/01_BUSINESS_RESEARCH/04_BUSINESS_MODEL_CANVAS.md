All cells are **PROPOSED hypotheses** pending market, legal and finance decisions.

| Element | Hypothesis and validation |
|---|---|
| Segments | Vehicle sellers, verified commercial buyers, later consumer buyers and service partners. Validate each segment's needs and willingness to pay. |
| Value | Seller receives supervised inspection and a choice among qualifying bids; dealer receives accessible supply and evidence. A bid is not guaranteed sale. |
| Channels | English web, search content, consented CRM, branches and dealer onboarding. Verify acquisition cost and reach by cohort. |
| Relationships | Assisted branch and transaction service, self-service status, human support and reasoned dispute handling. |
| Revenue | A disclosed transaction service fee is a candidate; subscription, inspection and partner services require separate approval. No rate is assumed. |
| Key activities | Seller acquisition, identity checks, inspection, dealer approval, auction oversight, contract and settlement coordination. |
| Key resources | Qualified inspectors, branch slots, dealer network, reliable bid ordering, protected documents and legal templates. |
| Partners | Identity, payments, communications, transport, legal and financing providers where authorized; contractual and regulatory status unverified. |
| Costs | Acquisition, facilities, labor, inspection equipment, customer support, cloud/security, insurance, compliance, dispute handling and provider fees. |

## Transactions and boundaries
Planning scenario: seller requests an inspection; approved dealer bids; seller chooses; named parties sign approved contracts and perform external settlement; the platform tracks verified obligations and transfer. No platform custody, escrow, ownership or credit is implied. An alternate inventory-purchase model changes title, working capital, tax, warranty and inventory risk; compare separately before adoption. See [detailed operations and illustration](../01_BUSINESS_MODEL_AND_OPERATIONS.md).

## Acceptance
Sponsor and domain owners validate each relationship, payer, fee trigger, liability, cost allocation and market-specific regulatory requirements. Economics must use real cohort data rather than synthetic discovery figures. An unapproved canvas is not authorization to transact.

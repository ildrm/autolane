## Opportunity hypothesis
**PROPOSED:** Sellers face friction in assessing vehicle value, verifying counterparties and coordinating inspection, payment and transfer. Dealers need reliable, appropriately priced supply and consistent condition information. Managed evidence and competitive bidding may reduce these frictions, but no demand, price premium or unit economics is established.

## Segmentation and evidence plan
| Segment | Job and barrier | Research method | Testable exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual sellers | Understand value, avoid unsafe meetings, complete transfer. | Interview recent sellers; observe appointment/offer prototype. | Users can explain quote, decision and payment responsibility without coaching. |
| Independent dealers | Find vehicles, assess condition and bid within authority. | Interviews and mock auctions by vehicle class and region. | Dealers identify report limitations and can submit/reconcile a bid. |
| Branch staff | Process vehicles safely within finite capacity. | Time-and-motion observation and exception drills. | A shift model supports peak demand without concealing uninspected items. |
| Consumer buyers | Discover vehicles and evaluate total cost. | Search usability studies and offer comprehension tests. | Buyers distinguish listing availability, price and applicable terms. |

## Sizing model and validation
Do not invent TAM. For the chosen geography, estimate annual addressable transfers = verified registrations or transfer volume × in-scope used-vehicle share × geographically reachable share; serviceable opportunity = addressable transfers × segment fit × feasible operational capacity. Revenue opportunity = expected completed transactions × net earned fee plus separately validated services, less tax and refunds. Specify source year, range, overlap and uncertainty; do not multiply incompatible datasets or call transaction value platform revenue.

Test liquidity by segment: eligible dealers per auction, no-bid rate, bid dispersion, seller-decision and close cohorts. Report aged cohorts and confidence ranges; select pilot thresholds before launch. Decisions: jurisdiction, target segment and evidence budget. Acceptance: sponsor reviews sourced low/base/high sizing and operational capacity before claiming an investment case.

## Conscious boundaries, not concealed completion claims

Actual platform funds movement, credit-backed bidding, destructive identity merging and provider-specific external protocols are not silently specified as available. Their chosen disabled/manual/adapter boundary is explicit and testable. Unapproved market-dependent terms cannot be made complete by inventing a jurisdiction. Final visual assets, usability evidence, performance measurements and operational exercises are future design/build/release deliverables; the current phase provides their production specification and acceptance criteria.

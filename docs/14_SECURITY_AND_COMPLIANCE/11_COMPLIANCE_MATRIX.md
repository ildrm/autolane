## Status and applicability

No launch jurisdiction, regulated entity or licensing model has been selected. This matrix is an issue inventory, not a legal opinion or a claim of compliance. Qualified local counsel and operational owners must identify actual authorities, primary legal sources, applicable thresholds and effective dates for every market before launch.

| Area | Decision/evidence needed | Control owner | Release condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace intermediation and dealer sales | Parties, representation, vehicle ownership, consumer rights and disclosure | Legal + product | Approved agreement and operational process. |
| Payments, escrow and custody | Fund flow, provider licenses, custody, reconciliation, refunds and safeguarding | Legal + finance | Approved money flow, licensed provider where required and signed contracts. |
| Consumer and dealer financing | Lender identity, referral disclosures, underwriting and advertising rules | Legal + credit | Approved provider agreement and consent/disclosure. |
| Identity and anti-fraud | Required checks, record handling, sanctions and appeals as applicable | Compliance + security | Documented obligations and review process. |
| Privacy and data transfers | Lawful basis, notices, rights, retention, residency and processors | Privacy | Reviewed data inventory, notices and processing terms. |
| E-signature, contracts and title transfer | Valid signature and ownership evidence, jurisdiction-specific forms | Legal + branch operations | Approved template, verification and handover workflow. |
| Tax, invoicing and accounting | Taxable event, payer, invoices, recognition and reporting | Finance + tax | Signed tax and accounting policy plus reconciliation tests. |
| Inspection, claims and warranty | Professional standards, remedies, exclusions and liability | Inspection + legal | Reviewed checklist, public claims and dispute policy. |
| Accessibility and content | Applicable accessibility obligations, advertising and structured data accuracy | Design + legal | Reviewed conformance target and content controls. |

## Acceptance

Each row must link to primary legal authority, applicability analysis, owner, approved policy, evidence of testing and review date before relevant functionality is launched. Escalate conflicting source interpretations; do not infer the new product's obligations solely from the reference company's terms.

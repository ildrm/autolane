## Purpose and evidence

Identify personal data for seller and buyer profiles, dealer representatives, employee records, vehicle identifiers, contracts, support, analytics and communications. For each processing purpose document lawful basis, data minimization, recipient, retention, deletion and jurisdiction. Consent is not assumed to be the lawful basis for every service; optional analytics and marketing require the applicable choice and must not be bundled with essential service acceptance.

Record consent or preference as purpose, policy/notice version, timestamp, method, actor, region and withdrawal history. Make revocation as accessible as granting where required. On withdrawal stop future optional processing and notify processors as required; determine effects on already processed information and legal retention with counsel. Essential service messages use their own approved basis. Provide understandable access, correction and deletion request intake, identity verification, deadline tracking and response records; do not promise a universal deadline before the jurisdiction is chosen.

## Controls and acceptance

Set nonessential tracking to disabled until required choice is recorded; reconcile downstream opt-out across web, messaging and analytics. A customer can see and change channel preferences without accessing another account. Testing demonstrates that withdrawal stops future optional events, that legally retained evidence is not deleted prematurely, and that a privacy request has an owner and auditable completion or exception. A privacy impact assessment is required before adding financing, behavioral profiling or cross-border data sharing.

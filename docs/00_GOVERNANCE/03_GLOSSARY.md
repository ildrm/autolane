# Domain glossary

| Scope | Domain glossary; English-first automotive marketplace documentation |

| Term | Definition and important distinction |
|---|---|
| Seller request | A seller's case for selling a specific vehicle; not a contract or proof of ownership. |
| Lead | A contact and sales opportunity; one lead can relate to several requests. |
| Vehicle identity | Record of the vehicle and its identifiers; verification of owner authority is separate. |
| Appointment | Reserved branch capacity for a time interval, not proof of attendance. |
| Inspection report version | Immutable snapshot of checklist findings, limitations and evidence at a specific time; a later correction creates a new version. |
| Valuation | Indicative range and confidence based on available data; not necessarily a binding offer or reserve. |
| Auction | A time-bounded process for eligible bidders under published terms; a result alone does not prove a completed sale. |
| Bid | A bidder's submitted amount with an authoritative acceptance or rejection decision; only accepted bids participate in ranking. |
| Reserve | A minimum qualifying condition if approved; policy governs disclosure and outcome. No default reserve behavior is authorized here. |
| Auction outcome | Result of close, such as qualified highest bid or no qualifying bid; distinct from a seller decision. |
| Seller decision | Explicit acceptance or rejection of a presented offer version; silence is not acceptance absent lawful approved terms. |
| Contract | Agreement between named parties with reviewed terms and evidence of execution; its legal effect is jurisdiction-specific. |
| Obligation | A party's recorded duty to pay an amount or perform an action; not proof of payment. |
| Settlement | Fulfilment and verification of financial obligations under selected model; an uploaded receipt alone is not confirmed settlement. |
| Reconciliation | Matching independent authoritative evidence to expected obligations, with discrepancies retained for review. |
| Title and custody | Legal ownership and physical control of a vehicle, respectively; neither implies the other or paid funds. |
| Dealer organization | Approved business and its explicitly authorized members; approval of one person does not automatically authorize every member to bid. |
| Consent | A recorded permission for an identified use and version; it must not be conflated with a contractual basis or all marketing permissions. |
| Published listing | Consumer-visible vehicle representation, separately authorized from dealer-only auction access. |
| Policy version | Effective-dated approved set of terms or configuration; changing a policy must not silently rewrite a historical transaction. |

Every definition is a proposed cross-team vocabulary, not a statement of local law; conflicts with approved legal terms require a recorded change.

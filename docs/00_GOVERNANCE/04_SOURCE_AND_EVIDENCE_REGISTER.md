## Competitor source additions
Accessed 2026-11-15: [Carvana seller journey](https://www.carvana.com/sell-my-car) describes an online offer and vehicle review; [CarMax selling explanation](https://www.carmax.com/articles/how-to-sell-your-car-to-carmax) describes online/in-store appraisal and offer terms; [Manheim pre-sale policy](https://site.manheim.com/en/marketplace-policies/us-policies/pre-sale-certification-policy.html) describes limits of a single inspection. These are first-party statements for comparison, not proof of similar local legal arrangements. Preserve the source, location, date, limitations and translation whenever promoting a fact to a new artifact.

## Evidence quality and refresh
Sources: first-party public pages for stated offerings; signed contracts and market counsel for actual obligations; primary user research with consent for user needs; recorded operational metrics for baseline performance. Track unavailable wizard and authenticated dealer content as unverified. Recheck material claims at each commercial decision, with date and reviewer; mark changed or inaccessible evidence rather than silently deleting it. Proprietary or authenticated systems must not be accessed without authority.

## Mandate and outcomes
**PROPOSED:** Create an English-first managed vehicle marketplace connecting sellers to approved commercial buyers, beginning with booking, inspection, dealer auction, seller decision, contract coordination, settlement tracking and closure. Product, legal and finance have not approved a launch jurisdiction or commercial model. No live customer transaction is authorized by this document.

| Outcome | Evidence for acceptance | Accountable owner |
|---|---|---|
| Coherent vehicle transaction | An eligible case can traverse the proposed loop with each handoff and exception owned. | Product director |
| Fair, auditable bidding | A valid bid receives an authoritative result; simultaneous bids cannot create two winners; rejected and duplicate bids are explainable. | Auction operations |
| Accurate completion | Auction outcome, seller consent, payment evidence, transfer and custody are independently represented. | Finance and legal |
| Inclusive experience | English LTR interface, keyboard and screen reader tasks, and reduced-motion option pass accessibility review. | Design lead |

## Constraints and scope
Documentation only; no implementation in this phase. Use original brand and licensed assets. Configurable jurisdiction, currency, time zone, taxes and retention are required; a chosen interface language does not determine the market. Phase 1 is a managed dealer loop, scoped support, identity, audit and notifications. Consumer listings, sourcing and editorial growth follow; finance, warranty and logistics depend on approved partners and policies. High-fidelity 3D and ML are later experiments, not launch prerequisites.

## Governance and gates
The sponsor owns investment; product owns scope; operations owns branch reality; finance and legal approve model, fee, custody and jurisdiction; architecture owns consistency; security owns controls; QA owns independent evidence. Proposals require review and recorded acceptance; a virtual review does not equal stakeholder signoff. P0 decisions DEC-01–DEC-07 in the master index block implementation readiness. Release gates require documented product, UX, visual, motion, architecture, security, QA, SEO, operations and handoff approvals.

## Dependencies, risks and acceptance
Critical dependencies are an approved target market, transaction terms, human inspection capacity, dealer supply, identity capability and settlement evidence. Risks include thin liquidity, stale inspection reports, bid race, payment ambiguity and unauthorized disclosure. Acceptance: named accountable owners review scope and decisions, approve budget and success measures, and confirm no P0 remains before approving an implementation baseline. Estimates remain hypotheses until supported by research.

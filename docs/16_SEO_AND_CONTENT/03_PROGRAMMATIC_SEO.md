# Programmatic SEO

**ID:** 16/03_PROGRAMMATIC_SEO · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED, subject to content, legal and engineering approval · **Owner:** Head of SEO · **Reviewers:** Content, product, privacy, engineering · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Eligible page families
Programmatic pages are editorial products built from verified structured data, not an automatic cross-product of every make, model, trim, city and filter. Candidate templates: make overview, model overview, city marketplace, model-in-city, model pricing guide and branch; eligibility is evaluated before publication. No claim about search volume or available cars exists until measured.

| Gate | Minimum publish condition | Failure action |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Research supports a distinct recurring user intent; SEO reviewer records source/date. | Draft or suppress; do not fabricate keyword volume. |
| Unique usefulness | Original editorial introduction plus relevant specifications, locally applicable guidance and accessible related links; not swapped names only. | Return to editor; keep noindex until reviewed. |
| Data quality | Valid taxonomy, licensed data, source date, no contradictory price, and meaningful current content or inventory. | Mark stale; remove price or suppress. |
| Coverage | City exists in approved service footprint; displayed branch and availability verified. | Do not publish misleading local service page. |
| Canonical | One stable URL; no duplicate token/filter permutations; metadata and breadcrumbs match. | Consolidate or redirect only when equivalent. |
| Legal/accessibility | Finance/warranty claims approved, sourceable and visible; WCAG review. | Block publication until resolved. |

## Lifecycle and monitoring
Draft → data validation → editorial and legal review → publish/indexable → scheduled revalidation → stale/unpublish. Set ownership and review date for every page. A temporary zero-result category can remain useful if editorial guidance remains accurate and explicitly says inventory is unavailable; otherwise noindex or retire after review. Monitor indexed pages per family, duplicate canonicals, stale data, crawl anomalies, clicks and qualified conversion; sample 20 pages per template or all when fewer and inspect meaning, sources, grammar and actual inventory. Indexing is not itself a success metric; avoid publishing low-value pages simply to increase URL count.

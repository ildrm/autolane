# Content strategy

**ID:** 16/05_CONTENT_STRATEGY · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED, subject to content, legal and engineering approval · **Owner:** Head of SEO · **Reviewers:** Content, product, privacy, engineering · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Editorial pillars
| Pillar | User question / formats | Required evidence and next action |
|---|---|---|
| Selling | How does inspection, offer, seller choice and handover work? | Approved workflow, illustrative fee disclosure and support; Start selling. |
| Buying and comparison | How do models differ and how can condition be assessed? | Current specification sources, model-year context, inspection limits; browse consumer inventory. |
| Valuation | What affects the estimate, and what does confidence mean? | Source dates, methodology limits, currency and explicit estimate; request valuation. |
| Inspection and ownership | What do technical findings mean and what should a buyer ask? | Expert review and distinction between findings and warranty; view guide. |
| Finance | What options may a licensed provider offer? | Applicable provider/terms, example assumptions and legal review; no approval guarantees. |
| Local branch | Where and when can I visit? | Location, confirmed hours, accessibility, contact and service status; Book appointment. |
| Automotive news | What changed and why does it matter? | Primary sources, date, author, corrections, no invented market data. |

## Production
Editorial brief records audience, intent, source URLs and access dates, original angle, approved factual claims, page owner, legal risk, target related page and review date. Draft in English with clear headings, source attribution and accessible images. Automotive expert validates technical facts, SEO validates intent and linking, legal validates finance/contract/warranty and privacy, editor publishes. Refresh when major facts change and on a schedule appropriate to volatility; record `reviewed_at` separately from original publication and change dates. Keep a correction log for material errors.

## Metrics and acceptance
Track qualified organic visits, article-to-relevant-action progression and confirmed downstream conversions only where lawful and consented. Avoid presenting clicks or model predictions as evidence of a sale. No unsupported testimonials or copied reference images. A page lacking sourceable information is held in draft, not published with placeholders.

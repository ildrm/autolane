# Internal linking

**ID:** 16/08_INTERNAL_LINKING · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED, subject to content, legal and engineering approval · **Owner:** Head of SEO · **Reviewers:** Content, product, privacy, engineering · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Navigation graph
Every important approved public page should be reachable by ordinary HTML links from an appropriate parent within a small number of meaningful steps; no fixed click-depth guarantee is claimed. Main nav links to selling, buying, value, branches, how it works and help. Make pages link to model pages; model pages link to relevant buying guides and available consumer inventory; city pages link to real branches and relevant listings; guide pages link to relevant service and references. Branch detail links to appointment entry. Vehicle detail links to the appropriate model and buyer guide without revealing private dealer lots.

## Editorial and lifecycle rules
Anchor text describes destination (“View inspection guide”), not repeated generic “click here”; avoid keyword stuffing. A contextual link must match user intent and destination status, be keyboard accessible and not depend on client-side click handlers. Breadcrumbs reflect a single meaningful hierarchy; multiple logical paths may use related links rather than fabricated ancestry. Remove or update links to withdrawn listings/closed branches, and redirect only when a genuinely equivalent successor exists. Sitemaps complement internal links; they do not replace navigation.

## Verification
Weekly automated crawl checks orphaned indexable URLs, 4xx/5xx, loops, redirects, anchors, canonical destinations and nofollow decisions; review a sample manually for relevance and accessibility. Do not expose privileged URLs, tokens or personal details in public links. SEO owns remediation, editorial owns context, engineering owns link correctness.

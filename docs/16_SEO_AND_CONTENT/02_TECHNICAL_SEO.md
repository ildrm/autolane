# Technical SEO

**ID:** 16/02_TECHNICAL_SEO · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED, subject to content, legal and engineering approval · **Owner:** Head of SEO · **Reviewers:** Content, product, privacy, engineering · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Rendering and crawl
Serve meaningful primary content, navigation, title, canonical, robots directives, price/availability when public, and key links in initial HTML; enhance interactions progressively. Server rendering is a **PROPOSED** approach and does not require a specific framework. Inspect source HTML and rendered output; Google describes [JavaScript search basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics), but Google behavior is not a guarantee for all engines. Public content must not require a login, click or client-only API to appear. Never render private dealer auction material to crawlers.

## Indexing signals and lifecycle
Use one HTTPS canonical URL, self-canonical on canonical pages, redirects for permanent moves and canonical tags for duplicate variants; canonical is a hint, not an access control. See [canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls). List only 200-status, indexable, canonical URLs in grouped sitemaps and update `lastmod` only on material changes; [sitemaps](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview) do not guarantee indexing. Paginated result pages have unique URLs, working links and self-canonical when content differs; no infinite-scroll-only access. Avoid indexing uncurated faceted combinations and internal search results.

Private routes require authorization regardless of robots directives; return no sensitive body to unauthenticated clients. `noindex` requires a crawler to access the directive; [robots.txt](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/intro) is not secrecy, and [noindex](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing) is not usable to restrict access. For removed private information revoke public access first, then manage removal. Expired consumer listings follow [indexation rules](09_INDEXATION_RULES.md) instead of redirecting every listing to a generic home page.

## Performance and verification
Target p75 field LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1 across mobile/desktop segments as documented by [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals); these are proposed release objectives, not measurements. Target initial marketing route JS ≤170 KB compressed and above-fold image ≤150 KB as lab budgets; defer noncritical scripts, reserve image dimensions, avoid autoplay video and third-party scripts without consent. Test with no JS, slow network, real crawler inspection, sitemap/canonical validator, redirects and structured-data validator; monitor errors and rollback faulty templates. Future hreflang must use reciprocal real equivalent URLs only after genuine localization and region review.

# SEO strategy

**ID:** 16/01_SEO_STRATEGY · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED, subject to content, legal and engineering approval · **Owner:** Head of SEO · **Reviewers:** Content, product, privacy, engineering · **Updated:** 2026-11-15

## Objectives and scope
Acquire relevant, informed visitors to vehicle selling, buying, valuation, branches, inspections and dealer services; measure qualified downstream activity rather than rankings alone. Target English-speaking users without assuming a jurisdiction. Actual demand, search volume, business footprint and competitors need research and are **UNVERIFIED**. No content, ranking or traffic is claimed to exist.

## Search-intent architecture
| Intent | Page family and desired next action | Eligibility and owner |
|---|---|---|
| Sell a car | `/sell`, selling guides → start request. | Approved process, fees and conditions; product/legal. |
| Find vehicles | `/buy`, make/model/city and detail pages → inquiry. | Real, consented consumer inventory; inventory and SEO. |
| Estimate price | `/value`, model price guides → estimate. | Transparent valuation caveats and current source date; pricing. |
| Inspect/compare | inspection guides, model comparisons → understand condition. | Fact-checked original explanations; automotive editor. |
| Local branch | `/branches/{slug}` → view hours/book. | Verified address, hours, accessibility and open status; branch operations. |
| Finance | Financing guide → review available partner only where approved. | Legal review, lender identity, required disclosures, no approval guarantee. |
| Commercial buyer | `/dealers` → dealer application. | Approved eligibility and auction descriptions; dealer operations. |

## Approach and governance
Prioritize useful evergreen content and real inventory. Publish make/model/city pages only if editorial substance, unique demand, current data and navigation support them. Do not clone one paragraph across combinations or index empty filter pages. Build pages from lawful original content and properly licensed images. Reassess a published page after policy, vehicle availability or location changes. Search visibility does not override privacy, security or legal review. Detailed robots/indexation, canonical, expired listing and structured-data rules reside in the adjacent documents.

## Measurement and acceptance
Monthly report organic search clicks, impressions, indexed canonical pages, page quality, p75 performance and consent-permitted confirmed leads, bookings and dealer applications; segment brand/nonbrand and page family without asserting individual user identity. Baseline is unknown. Mark correlations between organic sessions and later conversions as attribution estimates, not causal results. Release gate: important indexable pages have unique human-reviewed content, self-consistent canonical/sitemap metadata, accessible server-rendered primary content, valid internal links and up-to-date structured data. Search engine indexing and rich results are not guaranteed.

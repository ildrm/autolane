## Reading the evidence

**VERIFIED** means the identified accessible source makes the stated claim, not that the claim has been independently audited. The primary pages are Persian; the English descriptions below are paraphrases, and legal interpretation requires qualified counsel and the governing original text. Public pages are mutable. **INFERRED** means a reasoned hypothesis from named evidence, not an observed internal feature. **PROPOSED** means a choice for the new product. **UNVERIFIED** means evidence was insufficient. **DECISION REQUIRED** means a consequential choice remains open.

The extraction of the seller wizard returned no readable content, and the dealer login returned a loading screen. This limits observations to their public entry points, not the interactive or authenticated experiences. No account was created and no access control was bypassed. The evidence below is not a current census, a representation of performance, or a license to reuse the reference company's branding or content.

## Evidence reconciliation and research actions

1. **Payment and credit:** E-04 is explicit within the seller terms, while E-05 markets a credit product and E-03 mentions a deposit. These sources may concern different contracts, participants or providers; they do not establish that explanation. Obtain current seller, dealer, financing and deposit agreements and map parties, funds and liabilities before selecting money movement. Until then, the new product must not claim to hold money, guarantee settlement, extend credit, or own vehicles.
2. **Guarantees:** distinguish an advertised inspection guarantee from coverage, exclusions and contractual remedy. Obtain the actual terms and claims procedure before copying any promise or establishing a product policy.
3. **Coverage counts:** retain each observed count with its source. Do not propagate a single current network figure from inconsistent pages; contact the reference company or validate its current directory if research needs a precise number.
4. **Auction semantics:** public copy confirms highest bid and subsequent seller agreement, but not the rules needed to implement a fair auction. Establish independent proposed rules and validate with legal and operations, rather than treating them as reference behavior.
5. **Restricted content:** research the public entry and descriptions only. Seek consent and authorized demonstrations if actual interface details become necessary. Do not attempt private endpoints or circumvent authentication.

## Risks, acceptance and review

**Evidence risks:** stale pages, marketing overstatement, extraction of dynamic content, translation error, jurisdiction-specific terms, and overgeneralizing channel-specific statements. Preserve original source URLs and date; request qualified Persian and legal review for transaction-critical interpretations.

**Acceptance criteria:** each reference-specific claim maps to a source above; a claim of fact about a source is separated from product recommendation; all 30 mandated questions have a documented answer or explicit gap; the wizard and authenticated dealer surfaces are marked inaccessible; no precise internal technical architecture or proprietary algorithm is asserted. Reviewers should re-open sources and check translations and links before business or legal sign-off.

**Open decisions:** target launch jurisdiction; seller/dealer legal agreements; money custody and settlement responsibilities; fee schedule; ownership and default model; financing provider; warranty coverage; and authorized use of data. These are blocking business decisions, not resolved reference facts.

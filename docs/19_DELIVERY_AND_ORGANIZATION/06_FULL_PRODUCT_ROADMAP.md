## Phased product outcomes
Timings below are planning ranges for a balanced team and start **after** material prerequisites are agreed; they are not dates or commitments. Phase 2+ estimates are broad, non-additive where discovery/integration proceeds in parallel, and must be re-estimated after pilot evidence.

| Phase | Outcome and included scope | Exclusions / dependencies | Indicative effort and launch evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Foundational MVP | Seller → booking → inspection → reviewed valuation → approved dealer auction → decision → contract, settlement and transfer tracking. Includes essential support, access, audit and operations. | No unapproved funds custody, lending, warranty, proxy bids or advanced ML. DEC-01–07 and branch readiness are gates. | Balanced team roughly 10–15 calendar months including controlled pilot; full-loop, exceptional-path, concurrency and recovery UAT. |
| 2 Commercial marketplace | Consumer inventory and inquiry, search, sourcing, account dashboards, CRM automation, editorial SEO and reporting. | Separate listing publication from private auctions; no guaranteed availability. Depends on reliable inventory and consent. | Initial planning range 4–7 months after MVP for meaningful subset; search freshness, accessibility, editorial and conversion tests. |
| 3 Advanced operations | Evaluate lender referral, logistics partners, warranty, dealer credit and cross-branch analytics. | Each service requires contract, licensing, privacy, operating and provider review; do not activate a bundle by default. | Initial range 6–12 months, modular delivery; provider sandbox, reconciliation, claim and incident drills. |
| 4 Premium differentiation | Evaluate 3D, improved market intelligence, ML-assisted valuation and personalization. | Only with user value, training data rights, model governance, accessibility and performance evidence; humans retain review where consequential. | Initial range 6–12 months for first approved bets; controlled experiments, drift and performance reviews. |

Each phase has a product owner, engineering/design/QA/operations coverage, legal/security review, measurable success baseline, training, monitored release and rollback. Later phases need financing/provider and market evidence before staffing or dates are committed. Risks include low buyer liquidity, provider dependency, privacy, incorrect valuations and operational growth ahead of capacity. Phase transitions require independent gate approval; no outcome is automatically promised by these ranges.

## Phase 1 — managed transaction loop
**Outcome:** an eligible seller can request inspection, schedule, receive a reviewed offer from an authorized dealer auction, choose whether to proceed, and have contract, settlement evidence, transfer and handover tracked to a justified closure. Launch is limited to an explicitly approved jurisdiction, branch cohort and trained staff.

| Capability | Included | Explicit phase-1 boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Seller and branch | English intake, consent, lead, slot reservation, reschedule, check-in, support | No automatic transfer or completion on an unanswered offer. |
| Vehicle and inspection | Vehicle identity, configurable checklist, photos, supervisor-published immutable report, correction | Inspection warranty is not promised; proprietary reference checklist is not copied. |
| Valuation and auction | Human-reviewed pricing, verified dealers, bid/close with authoritative ordering and audit, seller decision | Proxy bids, credit, extensions and deposits disabled absent explicit approval. |
| Transaction | Contract coordination, external settlement evidence and independent reconciliation, title and handover status | No custody, escrow, disbursement, lending or guaranteed payment inferred. |
| Foundation | Identity, scoped permissions, audit, notifications, accessibility, telemetry, monitored queues and recovery | No unapproved use of personal data; external provider actions require contracts. |

**Dependencies:** DEC-01–07, documented business rule version, complete contracts, representative users, provider availability, staff and budget. **Team:** balanced staffing is a planning scenario, with product, research/design, engineering, platform, QA, security, operations, finance and legal coverage; see team structure. **Risks:** bid inconsistency, dealer default, insufficient liquidity, inspection quality and settlement/transfer disputes; response owners and rollback are required.

**Pilot acceptance:** at least one scripted seller-to-closure journey and its rejection, no-bid, outage, default and discrepancy alternatives pass on the intended release candidate; concurrent bids and close exhibit correct ordered results; all restricted actions have authorization and audit evidence; legal, finance, operations, security, accessibility, QA and SRE approve their gates; support and incident coverage is active. Baseline cohort completion, bidder participation and discrepancy rate are measured with named denominators. No numerical conversion target is asserted before baseline. Exit is a recorded go/no-go, not a calendar date.

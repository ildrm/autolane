## Scenario planning, assumptions and arithmetic
All numbers are **illustrative assumptions**, not supplier quotes, confirmed headcount, actual cost or a forecast. USD is used solely as a comparable planning unit, not a launch-currency choice. Team allocation is in [team structure](01_TEAM_STRUCTURE.md); calendar ranges apply to MVP through a controlled pilot after project decisions and staffing mobilization. Example blended fully-loaded cost is **USD 10,000–16,000 per FTE-month**, including employer/contractor delivery costs but not indirect items below. Calculate low = FTE × low months × 10,000; high = FTE × high months × 16,000.

| Scenario | Average FTE | Indicative MVP months | Team effort FTE-months | Direct delivery labor estimate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Lean senior | 8.0 | 14–20 | 112–160 | USD 1.12m–2.56m |
| Balanced professional | 16.0 | 10–15 | 160–240 | USD 1.60m–3.84m |
| Full enterprise | 30.5 | 9–14 | 274.5–427 | USD 2.745m–6.832m |

These are scenario envelopes, **not** additive components and not evidence larger teams always deliver faster. The broad overlap covers unknown scope and coordination; concurrent dependencies and learning can extend all ranges. Apply a planning contingency of 20–35% to direct labor only after identifying risks: lean USD 1.344m–3.456m, balanced USD 1.92m–5.184m, enterprise USD 3.294m–9.223m (rounded). This is not an approved budget and should not be double counted with supplier risk premiums.

**Excluded from these totals:** branch fit-out and operating payroll, customer acquisition, licensing and regulator fees, lender/provider charges, transaction processing and payment costs, cloud and software usage, tax, legal opinions and litigation, vehicle photography rights, insurance, physical logistics, FX and financing cost, and contingency for market-specific compliance. Infrastructure costs need a separate workload-based estimate. Documentation phase (3–5 FTE for 1–2 months) is **USD 30,000–160,000** at the same assumed unit cost, before exclusions; it is a separate estimate and should not be added if the work is already included in the chosen delivery scenario.

Sensitivity: at 16 FTE over 12 months, each USD 1,000/FTE-month change moves direct labor by USD 192,000; each additional calendar month at USD 13,000 changes it by USD 208,000 before contingency. Validate blended rates, scope, capacity, staffing overlap, part-time expert availability, actual vendor offers and delivery throughput before budget approval. Record estimation date, confidence, owner and assumptions on every reforecast.

## Proposed release sequence
| Phase | Outcome and inclusion | Exclusions / dependency | Launch evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Foundational managed loop | Seller request, branch booking, verified inspection, valuation, approved dealer auction, seller choice, contracts and settlement tracking, support and closure. | No unapproved custody, lending, warranty or advanced ML; requires P0 legal/commercial decisions and trained staff. | Full-loop and failure UAT; audit, security and operations gates pass; pilot decision and incident owner assigned. |
| 2 Commercial marketplace | Consumer published inventory, search, sourcing, customer dashboards, CRM automation, content and reporting. | Consumer availability and auction inventory remain distinct; depends on operational data and publication policy. | Search accuracy, listing freshness, consent and editorial/indexation tests. |
| 3 Advanced operations | Partner finance, logistics, warranty, dealer credit, market data and branch optimization where approved. | No guaranteed loan or warranty; provider agreement and jurisdiction-specific scope required. | Provider sandbox, reconciliation and dispute drills; legal signoff. |
| 4 Differentiation | Evaluate 3D visualization, AI-assisted pricing, personalization and predictive operations. | No automatic high-impact decisions absent validation and human review. | Experiment shows measurable benefit without accessibility, privacy or performance regression. |

## Decision cadence
Review evidence after each phase rather than assigning invented calendar dates. Each release names business owner, staffing, dependencies, rollout cohort, operational training, monitoring, rollback and go/no-go authority. Suggested metrics: aged seller-to-close conversion, auctions with bids, qualified bidders, report corrections, customer task completion, payment discrepancies and support resolution. Set actual targets from measured baselines and approved service capacity. P0 decisions prevent an implementation-ready or market-launch declaration.

## Vision
**PROPOSED:** Make used-vehicle transactions understandable and dependable by combining credible inspection, verified commercial participation, transparent status and coordinated human service. The experience should communicate precision, trust and speed without presenting an indicative valuation as a guaranteed sale.

## Primary users and differentiated value
A seller submits vehicle details once, books inspection and chooses whether to proceed with an offer. An authorized dealer evaluates a versioned report, places a bid and sees its authoritative result. Branch and inspection teams process capacity and evidence, while legal and finance users coordinate distinct contracting, payment and transfer milestones. Consumer discovery and sourcing extend the ecosystem only after the foundational loop is reliable.

## Principles and boundaries
1. Seller choice and explainable prices; never create consent from silence.
2. An authoritative bid record and clear outage recovery; no animation or cached display determines a winner.
3. Accessible, English LTR interfaces and fast mobile workflows; respect reduced motion.
4. Human responsibility for contested evidence, identity exceptions and legal/financial decisions.
5. Minimize sensitive information; authorized users see only the case and purpose needed.
6. Original automotive identity and media, no reference-brand assets.
7. Jurisdiction, currency, taxes, date display and policy versions are configurable and legally reviewed.

## Success and non-goals
Measure qualified seller-to-close yield, offer coverage, unique eligible bidders, seller decision time, issue rates, reconciliation completeness and task success, with segment, cohort maturity and uncertainty. No target is a measured baseline. Do not assume custody of money, inventory ownership, a warranty, lending or an ML price estimate. Product, operations, finance, legal and design must review the vision before claiming approval.

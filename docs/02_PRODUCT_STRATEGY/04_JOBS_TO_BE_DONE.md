## Context → motivation → outcome
| Actor / situation | Job statement | Evidence of success | Failed or unmet condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seller uncertain of price | When selling, understand vehicle condition and likely value so I can judge an offer with confidence. | Seller explains estimate range, report caveats and actual offer as separate concepts. | No comparable data: provide review or abstain rather than a false exact price. |
| Seller with limited time | Arrange an inspection that fits my schedule and know what to bring. | Slot confirmed, local time and documents visible; change/cancel outcome clear. | Full branch or closure: offer alternative and record owner. |
| Dealer assessing supply | Review eligible vehicles and submit a bid with known status. | Bid acknowledgement and current authoritative leader visible. | Lost connection: recover recorded outcome before retry. |
| Inspector under time pressure | Record reliable evidence without losing unknown findings. | Mandatory findings and evidence reviewed before publication. | Missing test remains unknown; disputed report becomes a new reviewed version. |
| Finance reviewer | Match actual settlement evidence to obligations. | Difference explained, independent reviewer approves and history retained. | Partial/mismatched amount remains outstanding or disputed. |
| Support agent | Give a participant accurate next steps without exposing unrelated data. | Scoped case shows owner, status and next action. | Inability to verify identity routes to approved verification. |

## Prioritization
Foundational jobs precede discovery and personalization: no consumer search experience compensates for a wrong winner or unsupported settlement status. Validate job importance, frequency and current alternatives through research; job statements are proposed hypotheses. Acceptance: each high-priority job maps to a journey, screen, measurable outcome and tested failure state before release.

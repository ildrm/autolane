Personas below are **hypotheses**, not research findings; names represent role archetypes, not real people. Validate through consented interviews and observed tasks across market, ability, device and experience.

| Persona | Goal and context | Need / barrier | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maya, occasional seller | Sell a used vehicle with a predictable, safe process on mobile. | Explain valuation vs offer, required documents, fees, timing and ability to decline. | Observe whether she can book, locate status and identify who receives funds. |
| Daniel, dealer buyer | Evaluate multiple vehicles quickly and bid within delegated authority. | Compare inspection evidence, see authoritative bid status, avoid accidental duplicate action. | Measure recognition of bid acceptance, last update time and failure recovery. |
| Noor, branch coordinator | Manage limited slots and check customers in while assisting walk-ins. | Clear capacity, queue, absences, conflict alerts and scoped customer records. | Time real task sequence and assess errors during interruption. |
| Alex, inspector | Capture mechanical and cosmetic evidence using available devices. | Required/unknown findings, efficient photos, review and correction workflow. | Observe evidence quality and connectivity limitations. |
| Morgan, finance reviewer | Reconcile obligations against external evidence and protect payment details. | Clear discrepancies, segregation of duties and complete audit. | Simulate partial and mismatched payment without conflating status. |
| Lee, support specialist | Resolve a seller or dealer problem without unnecessary data exposure. | One case history, ownership, customer-safe status and escalation. | Assess handling of disputes and unauthorized data requests. |

## Inclusive research
Include keyboard/screen-reader users, low-bandwidth contexts, varied literacy, first-time and professional participants, and people who may need assistance. Avoid assuming all users own a particular device. Do not present a hypothetical demographic as a representative market segment. Acceptance: each persona has observed needs, contrary evidence and date before being used for investment or an irreversible design choice.

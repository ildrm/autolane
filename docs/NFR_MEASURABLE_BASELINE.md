## Measurement rules

All numeric values below are **proposed targets**, not measurements or guarantees. Product, operations and platform leads must approve coverage window, traffic assumptions and budget. For latency, use server-side duration from ingress acceptance to durable decision, excluding client-to-edge network; report count, p50/p95/p99, region and error rate. For availability, count an eligible authenticated synthetic operation successful only when the expected authoritative state is confirmed, and record planned maintenance separately rather than removing it without approval. Tests use production-like isolated staging with documented topology and [capacity assumptions](18_INFRASTRUCTURE_AND_DEVOPS/05_CAPACITY_PLANNING.md); real-user performance is measured in the field only under applicable privacy policy. No untested SLO becomes a customer promise.

## Open decisions and exceptions

Targets cannot override jurisdiction-specific obligations or the higher integrity invariant. Capacity and costs are illustrative, browser matrix and sample minimums need approval, and field metrics cannot be claimed before real users exist. For insufficient field data, use a documented lab prelaunch gate and reassess once the required sample exists; never describe a missing metric as passing. An exception needs named risk owner, reason, mitigation and expiry and cannot waive an open P0 legal or financial decision.
